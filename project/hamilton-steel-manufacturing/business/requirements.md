# Business Requirements

## Initial Scope

The solution must support the following production information:

- Production order
- Material
- Quantity
- Unit of measure
- Production date
- Shift
- Employee or team
- Work center
- Cost center
- Material cost
- Labor cost
- Production status

## Initial Reporting Requirements

- Total production quantity
- Material cost by order
- Labor cost by order
- Total production cost
- Cost per unit
- Production by work center
- Production by shift
- Planned versus actual cost
