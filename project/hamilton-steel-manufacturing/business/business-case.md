# Business Case

Hamilton Steel Manufacturing needs a reliable solution to capture production activities and connect operational data with financial analysis.

## Current Challenges

- Manual spreadsheet-based production tracking
- Limited cost visibility
- Disconnected operational and financial information
- Slow reporting cycles
- No single source of truth
- Limited readiness for advanced analytics and AI

## Target Outcomes

- Centralized production records
- Accurate material and labor cost analysis
- Standardized business rules
- Reusable data models
- Faster management reporting
- Foundation for SAP Datasphere and AI scenarios
